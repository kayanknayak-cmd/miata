# Satisfying 3D sim Shorts: what goes viral (snapshot 2026-10-28)

Data: 4,883 Shorts from 39 3D-sim channels (yt-dlp, channel Shorts tabs), plus each channel's
latest 15 uploads via RSS for recency. Format classified from titles (`classify.py`).

## Last 120 days (RSS, n=296): music marble 171K median, characters 102K, everything else 5K to 78K.

## Takeaways
1. Sound is the hook. Music-synced marbles beat every pure-physics format by 10x+ at the median.
2. Recognizable characters are the second lever. Generic squish/destruction is saturated.
3. Volume does not rescue weak formats: channels posting 7 to 35/week sit at 16K to 57K medians.
4. Distribution is extremely skewed (means 5 to 10x medians). Expect most uploads to flop.

## Caveats
Search-seeded sample (survivorship bias), rounded view counts, title-based classification,
lifetime views not age-normalized, one big channel (Music Marble) dominates its format.
